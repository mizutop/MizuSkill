# MizuSkill
###时间之不舍昼夜，世界之遥远无际。人之反复自我思考，什么才是我们想要的未来？是身不由己的被潮涌裹挟，还是主动选择掌握人生？
###Time is relentless, progressing continuously both day and night; the world is vast and inexhaustible. Individuals repeatedly engage in introspection: What future do we genuinely aspire to? Is it passively conforming to external forces beyond our control, or deliberately exercising agency to shape our own lives?
